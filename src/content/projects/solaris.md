An interactive 3D solar system simulation built with Three.js. Navigate the solar system, view textured planets at realistic positions, and explore from the Sun to Pluto.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Left mouse | Orbit the camera |
| Right mouse | Pan |
| Scroll wheel | Zoom |
| Keys 0–9 | Teleport to the Sun, Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, or Pluto |
| Gamepad right stick | Rotate the camera |

It is the third solar system I have built — after a [2014 simulator](https://ferrolho.github.io/solar-system-simulator/) and a [2016 CreateJS version](/projects/createjs-playground/).
