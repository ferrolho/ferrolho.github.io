An online gamepad tester: connect your gamepad and see its input displayed in real time, using the browser's Gamepad API with an Xbox wireless controller visualisation.

The controller view can also be **embedded** on any page with a copy-paste iframe snippet, resized automatically with [iframe-resizer](https://github.com/davidjbradshaw/iframe-resizer).
