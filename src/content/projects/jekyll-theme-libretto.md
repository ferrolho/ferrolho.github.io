**Libretto** is a responsive one-column Jekyll theme with classic styling and careful typographic details, based on the [Libretto WordPress theme](https://wordpress.org/themes/libretto/).

It is also the theme behind my Erasmus blog, [Away at Edinburgh](https://ferrolho.github.io/away-at-edinburgh/).
