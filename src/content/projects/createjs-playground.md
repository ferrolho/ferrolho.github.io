A set of small experiments with [CreateJS](https://createjs.com/) and the HTML5 canvas.

- **[Bacteria Simulator](https://ferrolho.github.io/createjs-bacteria-sim/)** — a living-organisms simulator using evolutionary genetic algorithms. ([Instructions and analysis](https://github.com/ferrolho/createjs-bacteria-sim/blob/gh-pages/README.md).) The little creatures are more like paramecia than bacteria, since they reproduce sexually and bacteria do not.
- **[Boids](https://ferrolho.github.io/createjs-boids/)** — a simple but mesmerising 2D [boids](http://www.red3d.com/cwr/boids/) simulation. Click anywhere to spawn a boid.
- **[Solar System](https://ferrolho.github.io/createjs-solar-system/)** — a minimalist simulation of the solar system to scale.
- **[Starry Night](https://ferrolho.github.io/createjs-starry-night/)** — my first try at building a CreateJS app.
